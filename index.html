Klemnu v spominIn memory of Klemen

1994 · 2026

Sin, partner in prijatelj, na katerega si se lahko vedno zanesel. A son, a partner, and a friend you could always count on.

Klemnova zgodbaKlemen's story

Klemen je bil ljubeč partner, sin, brat in stric, dragocen prijatelj in sodelavec, navdušen popotnik ter strasten padalec. 17. julija 2026 nas je mnogo prezgodaj zapustil. Ta stran je posvečena spominu nanj.

Klemen was a loving partner, son, brother, and uncle, a treasured friend and colleague, an avid traveler, and a passionate skydiver. On 17 July 2026 he left us far too soon. This page is dedicated to his memory.

Klemnova zgodbaKlemen's Story

Od Ljubljane do Züricha

From Ljubljana to Zurich

Klemen se je rodil v Ljubljani 11. januarja 1994, mami Zdenki in očetu Jožetu. Leta 1997 se je družini pridružila njegova sestra Alenka.

Klemen was born in Ljubljana on 11 January 1994, to his mother Zdenka and his father Jože. In 1997 his sister Alenka joined the family.

Obiskoval je Osnovno šolo Bičevje, nato Vegovo in zatem nadaljeval študij na Fakulteti za računalništvo in informatiko.

He attended Bičevje Primary School, then Vegova, and went on to study at the Faculty of Computer and Information Science.

Bil je vztrajen in delaven. Leta 2017 je odšel na Japonsko na izmenjavo v podjetje Google, kjer se je s svojim znanjem in delom dokazal. Leto pozneje, leta 2018, se je preselil v Zürich, kjer je delal kot programer in si gradil življenje v tujini.

He was persistent and hardworking. In 2017 he went to Japan on an exchange with Google, where he proved himself through his knowledge and his work. A year later, in 2018, he moved to Zurich, where he worked as a software developer and built a life abroad.

Leta 2024 je spoznal svojo partnerko Kai. Ob njej je odkrival nove strani sebe. Začel je kuhati in vrtnariti. Skupaj sta na balkonu v visokih gredah gojila zelišča in zelenjavo, načrtovala pa sta tudi, da se bosta naučila šivati. Veliko sta potovala, raziskovala svet in ustvarjala skupne načrte za prihodnost.

In 2024 he met his partner, Kai. With her, he discovered new sides of himself. He took up cooking and gardening. Together they grew herbs and vegetables in raised beds on their balcony, and they were also planning to learn how to sew. They traveled a great deal, explored the world, and made plans for the future together.

Čeprav je živel v tujini in ga je pot vodila v različne kraje, na dom in svoje bližnje ni nikoli pozabil. Družina mu je veliko pomenila. Nečak Oskar je bil vedno prvi, po katerem je vprašal. V njegovih zadnjih dneh pa je družina pozdravila še drugega nečaka, Lovra.

Although he lived abroad and his path took him to many places, he never forgot home or the people close to him. Family meant a great deal to him. His nephew Oskar was always the first person he asked about, and in his final days the family welcomed a second nephew, Lovro.

Klemen je bil preprost, ljubeč in skrben človek. Svojo naklonjenost je pogosto pokazal tiho, skozi dejanja, pozornost in skrb za ljudi, ki jih je imel rad. Prav takšnega ga bomo ohranili v spominu.

Klemen was a simple, loving, and caring person. He often showed his affection quietly, through his actions, his attentiveness, and his care for the people he loved. That is exactly how we will remember him.

Njegova največja strastHis greatest passion

Padalstvo

Skydiving

Padalstvo je bilo Klemnova največja strast. Leta 2017 je opravil padalski tečaj in od takrat je skakanju posvečal velik del svojega prostega časa. O padalstvu je rad pripovedoval, svoje navdušenje pa je z veseljem delil tudi z drugimi.

Skydiving was Klemen's greatest passion. In 2017 he completed his skydiving course, and from then on he devoted much of his free time to jumping. He loved to talk about it, and he gladly shared his enthusiasm with others.

V naslednjih letih je opravil več kot 1.500 skokov na različnih koncih sveta. Veliko časa je preživel tudi na letališču Šentvid pri Stični, kjer je zelo rad skakal in se družil s ljudmi, s katerimi ga je povezovala ljubezen do padalstva.

In the years that followed he made more than 1,500 jumps in different parts of the world. He also spent a lot of time at the Šentvid pri Stični airfield, where he loved to jump and to spend time with the people who shared his love of the sport.

Tam je opravil tudi svoj zadnji skok. V tolažbo nam je misel, da je bil takrat tam, kjer je bil najraje.

It was there that he made his final jump. It is a comfort to us to know that he was where he most loved to be.
