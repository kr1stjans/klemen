Klemnu v spominIn memory of Klemen

1994 · 2026

Sin, brat, partner in prijatelj, na katerega si se lahko vedno zanesel. A son, a brother, a partner, and a friend you could always count on.

Klemnova zgodbaKlemen's story

Klemen je bil ljubeč partner, sin, brat in stric, dragocen prijatelj in sodelavec, navdušen popotnik ter strasten padalec. 17. julija 2026 nas je mnogo prezgodaj zapustil. Ta stran je posvečena spominu nanj.

Klemen was a loving partner, son, brother, and uncle, a treasured friend and colleague, an avid traveler, and a passionate skydiver. On 17 July 2026 he left us far too soon. This page is dedicated to his memory.

Klemnova zgodbaKlemen's Story

Od Ljubljane do Züricha

From Ljubljana to Zurich

Klemen se je rodil v Ljubljani 11. januarja 1994, mami Zdenki in očetu Jožetu. Leta 1997 se je družini pridružila njegova sestra Alenka.

Klemen was born in Ljubljana on 11 January 1994, to his mother Zdenka and his father Jože. In 1997 his sister Alenka joined the family.

Obiskoval je Osnovno šolo Bičevje, nato Vegovo in zatem nadaljeval študij na Fakulteti za računalništvo in informatiko.

He attended Bičevje Primary School, then Vegova, and went on to study at the Faculty of Computer and Information Science.

Bil je vztrajen in delaven. Leta 2017 je odšel na Japonsko na izmenjavo v podjetje Google, kjer se je s svojim znanjem in delom dokazal. Leto pozneje, leta 2018, se je preselil v Zürich, kjer je delal kot programer in si gradil življenje v tujini.

He was persistent and hardworking. In 2017 he went to Japan on an exchange with Google, where he proved himself through his knowledge and his work. A year later, in 2018, he moved to Zurich, where he worked as a software developer and built a life abroad.

Leta 2024 je spoznal svojo partnerko Kai. Ob njej je odkrival nove strani sebe. Začel je kuhati in vrtnariti. Skupaj sta na balkonu v visokih gredah gojila zelišča in zelenjavo, načrtovala pa sta tudi, da se bosta naučila šivati. Veliko sta potovala, raziskovala svet in ustvarjala skupne načrte za prihodnost.

In 2024 he met his partner, Kai. With her, he discovered new sides of himself. He took up cooking and gardening. Together they grew herbs and vegetables in raised beds on their balcony, and they were also planning to learn how to sew. They traveled a great deal, explored the world, and made plans for the future together.

Čeprav je živel v tujini in ga je pot vodila v različne kraje, na dom in svoje bližnje ni nikoli pozabil. Družina mu je veliko pomenila. Nečak Oskar je bil vedno prvi, po katerem je vprašal. V njegovih zadnjih dneh pa je družina pozdravila še drugega nečaka, Lovra.

Although he lived abroad and his path took him to many places, he never forgot home or the people close to him. Family meant a great deal to him. His nephew Oskar was always the first person he asked about, and in his final days the family welcomed a second nephew, Lovro.

Klemen je bil preprost, ljubeč in skrben človek. Svojo naklonjenost je pogosto pokazal tiho, skozi dejanja, pozornost in skrb za ljudi, ki jih je imel rad. Prav takšnega ga bomo ohranili v spominu.

Klemen was a simple, loving, and caring person. He often showed his affection quietly, through his actions, his attentiveness, and his care for the people he loved. That is exactly how we will remember him.

Njegova največja strastHis greatest passion

Padalstvo

Skydiving

Padalstvo je bilo Klemnova največja strast. Leta 2017 je opravil padalski tečaj in od takrat je skakanju posvečal velik del svojega prostega časa. O padalstvu je rad pripovedoval, svoje navdušenje pa je z veseljem delil tudi z drugimi.

Skydiving was Klemen's greatest passion. In 2017 he completed his skydiving course, and from then on he devoted much of his free time to jumping. He loved to talk about it, and he gladly shared his enthusiasm with others.

V naslednjih letih je opravil 3.191 skokov na različnih koncih sveta. Veliko časa je preživel tudi na letališču Šentvid pri Stični, kjer je zelo rad skakal in se družil s ljudmi, s katerimi ga je povezovala ljubezen do padalstva.

In the years that followed he made 3,191 jumps in different parts of the world. He also spent a lot of time at the Šentvid pri Stični airfield, where he loved to jump and to spend time with the people who shared his love of the sport.

Tam je opravil tudi svoj zadnji skok. V tolažbo nam je misel, da je bil takrat tam, kjer je bil najraje.

It was there that he made his final jump. It is a comfort to us to know that he was where he most loved to be.

Njegovi poletiHis flights

Kot je nebo videl on

The Sky as He Saw It

Klemen je svoje skoke snemal in jih zbral na svojem kanalu. Šestindvajset posnetkov, nekaj več kot tri ure letenja, od Šentvida do Floride, Kolumbije in Empuriabrave. Klemen filmed his jumps and gathered them on his own channel. Twenty-six videos, a little over three hours of flying, from Šentvid to Florida, Colombia, and Empuriabrava.

Beseda njegovih padalskih prijateljevA word from his skydiving friends

»Mr. Smiley«

“Mr. Smiley”

Klemna smo spoznali leta 2018, na tečaju padalstva. Na začetku je bil nekoliko bolj tih, miren, zadržan človek, a zelo vedoželjen, delaven in že od samega začetka zelo strasten, kar se tiče padalstva – bil je tisti tip človeka, ki bi ga vsak inštruktor opisal kot idealnega učenca.

We met Klemen in 2018, on a skydiving course. At first he was a rather quiet, calm, reserved person, but very eager to learn, hardworking, and from the very beginning deeply passionate about skydiving – he was the kind of person any instructor would describe as the ideal student.

Kaj kmalu pa je iz učenca prerastel tudi v pravega prijatelja. Oktobra istega leta, po tem ko je že opravil padalsko licenco v Sloveniji, se je Klemen zaradi svoje poklicne kariere – zaposlil se je namreč pri Googlu – preselil v Švico. Tam je seveda nadaljeval svojo padalsko pot in opravil veliko skokov ter spoznal veliko novih ljudi, a se je kljub temu vedno rad vračal tudi k nam v Slovenijo, na »domače« letališče, k starim prijateljem – vsakič z nekoliko daljšo brado.

Before long he grew from a student into a real friend. In October of that same year, after he had already earned his skydiving licence in Slovenia, Klemen moved to Switzerland for his career – he had taken a job at Google. There he of course continued his skydiving path, made many jumps and met many new people, yet he always loved coming back to us in Slovenia, to our “home” airfield, to his old friends – each time with a slightly longer beard.

Klemen je imel vedno veliko željo po napredku, zato je svoje znanje najprej izpopolnjeval v vetrovnikih po svetu. Leta 2022 – mislim, da šele takrat zgolj zato, ker je v tistem letu kupil čisto svoje padalo, čelado in kombinezon, ki so se barvno in stilsko ujemali v njegovih barvah: rumeni, rjavi in sivi – pa se je odločil, da se udeleži tudi svojega prvega padalskega »skill campa« Fly 4 life. Kljub temu da je bilo vreme takrat precej slabo in skokov ni bilo ravno veliko, je bilo to dovolj, da je v Klemnu spodbudilo željo po še. Od tistega kampa naprej se je Klemen ekipi Fly 4 life na skill campih pridružil še vsaj 6-krat, včasih celo na dveh zaporednih kampih hkrati. There is no such thing as too much flying. Pot napredka ga je nato skupaj s prijatelji vodila širom sveta, od Italije, Nemčije, Španije, Švedske pa vse do ZDA, Kolumbije in Združenih arabskih emiratov. Tam je nabiral padalske izkušnje, znanje, predvsem pa je v tujini pridobil ogromno novih prijateljev ter z njimi ustvaril nešteto lepih spominov.

Klemen always had a strong desire to progress, so he first honed his skills in wind tunnels around the world. In 2022 – and I think only then, simply because that was the year he bought a parachute, helmet and suit all of his own, matched in colour and style to his own palette of yellow, brown and grey – he decided to attend his first Fly 4 life skill camp. Even though the weather was quite poor and there were not many jumps, it was enough to spark in Klemen a desire for more. From that camp onward he joined the Fly 4 life team at their skill camps at least six more times, sometimes even at two consecutive camps in a row. There is no such thing as too much flying. The path of progress then led him, together with his friends, across the world: from Italy, Germany, Spain and Sweden all the way to the USA, Colombia and the United Arab Emirates. There he gathered experience and knowledge, but above all he gained a great many new friends abroad and created countless beautiful memories with them.

Kljub temu da smo Klemna v začetku spoznali kot zelo tihega in zadržanega, pa smo sčasoma ugotovili, da v resnici ni bil čisto tak. Res je, da je bil njegov obraz pogosto resen – med letenjem celo do te mere, da si je zaradi tega prislužil vzdevek »Mr. Smiley«. S časom in z izkušnjami pa smo se naučili, da je ta njegov resni pogled največkrat samo krinka za kakšno pikro, zabavno opazko, ki je v začetku od njega še zdaleč ne bi pričakovali. V resnici je bil zelo zabaven, prizemljen in skromen človek, ki je bil vedno pripravljen pomagati, ne glede na to, ali je bil potreben zgolj nasvet ali pa kakšna roka v pomoč. Svojo delovno etiko nam je pokazal že čisto na začetku svoje padalske poti, na svojem prvem obisku Empuriabrave, kjer je vsak dan po skokih vneto pisal magistrsko nalogo. Pokazal nam jo je tudi s tem, da je vsak dan na letališču svoje padalo vedno zlagal sam in ga zložil najhitreje od vseh nas, večinoma brez da bi si sploh odpel kombinezon, ne glede na to, ali je bilo zunaj 15 ali 35 stopinj. Vedno brez kakršnegakoli nerganja ali pritoževanja. Predvsem pa nam je z vsem omenjenim pokazal svojo strast do padalstva.

Although we first came to know Klemen as very quiet and reserved, over time we realised that he was not quite like that at all. It is true that his face was often serious – while flying, so much so that it earned him the nickname “Mr. Smiley”. With time and experience, though, we learned that this serious look of his was most often just a disguise for some dry, funny remark that, in the beginning, we would never have expected from him. In truth he was a very funny, grounded and modest person, always ready to help, whether what was needed was simply a piece of advice or a hand. He showed us his work ethic right at the start of his skydiving path, on his first visit to Empuriabrava, where every day after jumping he diligently wrote his master’s thesis. He showed it to us too by always packing his own parachute at the airfield, every day, and packing it faster than any of us, mostly without even unzipping his suit, whether it was 15 or 35 degrees outside. Always without any grumbling or complaining. Above all, in all of this, he showed us his passion for skydiving.

Pogrešali te bomo, prijatelj naš dragi. Pogrešali bomo skupne večere, pogrešali bomo pogovore, druženje s teboj na tleh in v zraku. Pogrešali bomo skupna potovanja in dogodivščine v tujini in doma. Bilo nam je v največjo čast in veselje deliti si nebo s tabo …

We will miss you, our dear friend. We will miss our evenings together, we will miss the conversations, the time spent with you on the ground and in the air. We will miss our shared travels and adventures, abroad and at home. It was our greatest honour and joy to share the sky with you …

In kot pravimo med padalci: Blue skies, Klemen! Se vidimo tam zgoraj And as we say among skydivers: Blue skies, Klemen! See you up there
